## About
I am a transfer student from Haddon Township, New Jersey. In terms of my coding experience, in 2022 I had the idea for my startup, and over the past couple years, I built Dojo. Dojo is what I call a social operating system. Essentially its a user controlled social media platform, however it is in my eyes orders of magnitude beyond what else is out there. The main differentiator is the way people consume content. Where Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook are powered by blackbox algorithms, where behaviors are tracked, analyzed and stored, Dojo uses a content filter menu. The content filter menu lets the user add any word, topic, interest, and add it to their "list". They then get a slider for each topic to adjust if they want to see more or less of said topic. The way this works has been fine tuned over years of iterative design, and I would be happy to go into more detail about it if you would like.
